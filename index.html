Getting There

Travel & Stay

Where we’re staying

Le Petit Pali

8th Avenue · Carmel-by-the-Sea

We’re all staying together at Le Petit Pali, a storybook inn of river-rock walls, brick courtyards and window boxes. It’s a short walk to the beach, Ocean Avenue and the restaurants in the village.

We’ll share booking details and any group rate here as soon as they’re confirmed.

Open in Maps

Flying in

Monterey is the easiest option. The larger Bay Area airports usually have more flights and cheaper fares.

  • Monterey (MRY)~15 min
  • San José (SJC)~1.5 hrs
  • San Francisco (SFO)~2.5 hrs

Getting around

Carmel is very walkable, and the hotel, shops and Grasing’s are all close together. If you fly into SJC or SFO, rent a car. The drive down Highway 1 is beautiful.

Heads up: Carmel homes famously have no street addresses, so use business names in your maps app.

Free Time

Explore Carmel

A few of our favorite ways to spend Monday morning, or an extra day if you can stay.

Hike
Point Lobos State Reserve

Some of the most beautiful coastline anywhere. Easy trails, sea lions and hidden coves. Go early, because parking fills up fast.

Stroll
Ocean Avenue & the Village

Storybook cottages, art galleries, tucked-away courtyards and plenty of coffee shops.

Beach
Carmel Beach

White sand at the foot of Ocean Ave. It’s dog-friendly and perfect for a morning walk.

Drive
17-Mile Drive

Pebble Beach, the Lone Cypress and ocean views that don’t quit. Enter through the Carmel Gate.

History
Carmel Mission

A beautifully preserved 1771 mission with peaceful gardens, a few minutes from the ceremony beach.

Day Trip
Monterey Bay Aquarium

Twenty minutes up the road on Cannery Row. The kelp forest and sea otters are worth it.

Good to Know

Questions

What should I wear?

Beach formal for the wedding: think linen, light suits, flowy dresses and colors of the coast. The ceremony is on sand, so skip stilettos and bring flats, wedges or dressy sandals. Sunday’s dinner is casual.

What’s the weather like in May?

Carmel in early May is usually sunny and mild, around the mid-60s during the day and low 50s at night. It can be breezy on the beach and cool on the rooftop after sunset, so bring a layer or wrap.

Can I bring a plus-one or kids?
